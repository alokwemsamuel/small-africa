# Small Africa — Launch marketing plan

**Goal for the first 90 days:** fill the dining room on weekends, build a steady weekday lunch
trade, and get 1,000 people into the Interest Account so they keep coming back.

**Who we are talking to**

| Audience | What they want | Where they are |
|---|---|---|
| Lagos professionals (VI, Ikoyi, Lekki) | Somewhere impressive for dates, clients and celebrations | Instagram, Google Maps, WhatsApp |
| Food lovers and creators | Something new to try and post | TikTok, Instagram, food blogs |
| The Polish and wider European community | A taste of home; a place to bring Nigerian friends | Embassy and community groups, Facebook, WhatsApp |
| Companies | Client lunches, team dinners, end-of-year parties | LinkedIn, email, office managers |
| Delivery customers | Great food at home in Lagos traffic | Chowdeck, Glovo, the website |

**What makes us different, in one line:** *Three kitchens. One table.* Nigerian, Polish and
Continental, cooked properly, in one beautiful room, with a loyalty programme that pays
interest on dining credit.

Everything below can be run by one marketing person with help from the manager. The website
already supports it: coupon codes, the Interest Account, reservations, event enquiries and a
review system are all built in and managed from the admin panel.

> **Before launch:** replace the placeholder phone number, WhatsApp, address and social
> handles in **Admin → Settings** (see the [go-live checklist](GO-LIVE-CHECKLIST.md)).

## 1. Google Business Profile (week −2)

Most Lagos diners find restaurants on Google Maps. This is the most important free listing.

1. Create the profile at business.google.com with the exact name **Small Africa**, category
   **Restaurant** (secondary: *Fine dining restaurant*, *Lounge*, *Polish restaurant*,
   *Nigerian restaurant*, *Event venue*).
2. Verify the address (Google usually sends a code or asks for a short video).
3. Fill in everything: opening hours (Mon–Thu 12:00–23:00, Fri–Sat 12:00–00:00, Sun
   13:00–22:00), phone, website link, **Reserve** link to `/reservations`, **Order** link to
   `/menu`, menu link, price range, attributes (outdoor seating, live music, private dining,
   accepts cards).
4. Upload at least 20 photos: the room, the terrace, each kitchen's signature dishes, the bar
   and the team. Add new photos every week.
5. Post weekly *Updates* (events, Jollof Friday, a new dish) and add the launch offer.
6. Answer the common questions yourself in *Q&A* (parking, dress code, pork-free options,
   group bookings).

Suggested launch-only codes to add:

| Code | Offer | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| `THREEKITCHENS` | 15% off an order with a dish from each kitchen | First 30 days only |

**Rules of thumb:** never discount the whole menu for long; give codes an end date; use the
Interest Account (double points on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, top-up bonuses) instead of
discounts for regulars.

## 4. Interest Account launch push

The loyalty programme is the strongest reason to come back, so promote it on its own:

- **Founding Members:** the first 500 people to top up ₦50,000+ get the 5% top-up bonus (this
  is already the rule) plus a Founding Member welcome drink. Announce the number left each week.
- Table cards and receipts with a QR code to `/rewards`.
- Staff mention it at every bill: *"You'd have earned 450 points tonight. It takes a minute
  to join."*
- Monthly post: *"Our members earned ₦X in interest this month."* (Admin → Interest Account
  shows the total.)
- Remind people about **double points on Tuesday and Wednesday** to fill quiet days.
- **Referrals:** each member gets ₦2,000 credit per friend who joins and orders (up to 10 a
  month). Put each member's referral code on their account page and in emails.

## 6. Influencers

Pick **quality over follower count**: Lagos food and lifestyle creators whose audience lives
in VI, Ikoyi and Lekki and actually goes out to eat.

| Tier | Number | What they get | What we ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large food creators (100k+) | 2–3 | Dinner for two + a paid fee agreed upfront | 1 Reel + 3 Stories, their own code |
| Mid-size (10k–100k) | 6–8 | Tasting menu for two | 1 Reel or TikTok + Stories |
| Micro / community (2k–10k) | 15–20 | A meal, invited in small groups | Honest Stories |

- Spread visits across the first six weeks, not all on opening night.
- Brief them on the story (three kitchens, the Interest Account), not the script.
- Each gets a personal coupon code: **Admin → Coupons** shows how many orders each code brought.
- Ask for usage rights so their videos can be reshared and used in ads.
- Paid partnerships must say so (#ad / *Paid partnership* label), as Nigerian consumer
  protection rules (FCCPC) and Instagram both expect.

## 7. WhatsApp Business

Lagos customers order and book on WhatsApp. Use the **WhatsApp Business** app (or the API
later) with the number set in **Admin → Settings**; the website's contact page already links
to it.

- **Profile:** logo, address, hours, website, short description.
- **Catalogue:** the signature dishes with photos and prices, grouped by kitchen (Nigerian,
  Polish, Continental, Bar), each linking to its dish page on the website, where people can order
  and pay securely.
- **Quick replies:** `/menu`, `/book`, `/hours`, `/delivery` (zones and fees), `/events`.
- **Greeting and away messages** with the website link for orders after hours.
- **Labels** for new customer, event enquiry, corporate and VIP.
- **Status updates** daily: dish of the day, live music, events.
- **Broadcast list** (only people who agreed to it): one message a week at most.

## 8. Chowdeck and Glovo

Delivery apps reach people who don't know us yet; the website is better for repeat customers
(no commission, and they earn points).

- List a **shorter delivery menu** (dishes that travel well): Jollof, Ofada, Suya, Egusi,
  Pierogi, Bigos, Kotlet Schabowy, Chicken Alfredo, the desserts and Chapman. Leave out Lobster
  Thermidor and Beef Wellington.
- Prices on the apps can be a little higher than the website to cover commission (check each
  platform's rules).
- Put a small card in every delivery bag: *"Next time order direct and earn points. 10% off
  your first website order with WELCOME10."*
- Use the same photos as the website (from `design/photos`) so the brand looks the same.
- Check ratings on each app daily for the first month and answer complaints quickly.

## 9. Polish-community outreach

There are Polish and other European expatriates in Lagos who have nowhere to get pierogi or
żurek. They can become loyal regulars and bring Nigerian friends.

- Introduce the restaurant to the **Embassy of the Republic of Poland in Abuja** and the
  honorary consulate in Lagos; offer to host a reception or a national-day lunch
  (3 May, 11 November).
- Contact the **Polish-Nigerian business and community groups** (Facebook and WhatsApp groups,
  chambers of commerce), European schools, airline and oil-and-gas staff associations.
- A **"Taste of Home" evening** each month: Polish set menu, Polish beers, music. Invite the
  community first.
- Polish holidays on the calendar: **Tłusty Czwartek** (Fat Thursday: Pączki), **Wigilia**
  (Christmas Eve set menu), **Easter** (Żurek and Sernik).
- Menu and social posts can include the Polish dish names with simple English explanations,
  as the website does.

## 11. Email and SMS newsletter

- Collect consent at sign-up (the website asks for marketing consent separately, as Nigeria's
  data protection law, the NDPA 2023, requires). Only message people who said yes, and
  include an unsubscribe link in every email.
- **Email** (Brevo or Mailchimp; both have free plans): **twice a month**: new dishes, events,
  members' news and one offer. Subject lines short and specific (*"Pączki are back for Fat
  Thursday"*).
- **SMS** (Termii or similar, Nigerian sender ID): **only** for time-sensitive things: a
  same-day offer on a quiet evening, event reminders. At most twice a month.
- The website already sends the transactional emails (order confirmations and updates,
  booking confirmations), and puts interest payments, birthday rewards and win-back offers in
  each member's notification centre. Newsletters can repeat the best of these.

## 12. Reviews

Reviews decide where people eat. Aim for **100 Google reviews at 4.5★+ in 90 days**.

- On the bill folder and receipt: a QR code to the Google review page.
- Staff ask happy guests directly at the end of the meal (never offer rewards for reviews:
  Google forbids it).
- Signed-in customers can write reviews from their account on the website. Reviews are
  approved in **Admin → Reviews** before they appear, and approved ones show on the home page.
- **Reply to every review within 48 hours**, good or bad. For complaints: apologise, say what
  you've changed, and invite them to contact the manager directly.
- Share the best reviews on Instagram Stories every week (with the author's first name only).
- Check Tripadvisor, Chowdeck and Glovo ratings weekly.
