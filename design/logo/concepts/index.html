Small Africa — Logo Concepts

Four original directions, each shown on the three colourways from the brief, at favicon sizes and on a storefront sign. Lettering uses Cormorant Garamond and Manrope (OFL). The text in the chosen logo will be converted to outlines in the final export.

A — Continent Plate

A gold outline of the continent on a plate rim, with a fork and a leaf at its foot. The most literal of the four: it says "African dining" at a glance.

Small sizes: Storefront sign:

B — SA Monogram Crest

A serif monogram in a crest edged with a ring of diamonds. It reads like a hotel or private club, so it suits the 5-star positioning, and it holds up best as a 16px favicon.

Small sizes: Storefront sign:

C — Sun Wordmark

A refined serif wordmark with a small sun over the A and the tagline beneath. Elegant on signage and menus, but it needs a separate mark for the favicon.

Small sizes: Storefront sign:

D — Pattern & Papercut Badge

A zigzag ring inspired by Nigerian textiles around a bloom inspired by Polish wycinanki papercuts. It tells the Nigerian + Polish story most directly.
