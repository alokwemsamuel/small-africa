01 — Logo

The crest

An "SA" monogram in Cormorant Garamond, held in a crest ringed with 48 small diamonds. The diamonds nod to West African textile patterning; the star above marks the meeting point of three kitchens. Use the crest alone where space is tight, and the lockups wherever the name must be read.

01 — Logo

Please don't

The crest is the most recognisable thing we own. Keep it calm, gold, and uncluttered.

Stretch, squash or skew the crest.

Recolour it outside the brand palette.

Add shadows, glows, bevels or gradients.

Place it on busy patterns or photos without a calm backing.

Rotate it.

Fade it below full opacity on dark backgrounds.

Use gold on ivory: it fails contrast (2.1:1). Use obsidian.

S.Africa

Re-type, abbreviate or redraw the name.

04 — Motifs & voice

How we look and sound

Our own zigzag-and-diamond pattern, inspired by West African textile banding and the crest's diamond ring. Use it at 5–10% opacity behind headings, on packaging and menu covers. Pair with thin gold rules, generous whitespace and full-bleed food photography.

Photography: warm, low-key light; food fills the frame; real Lagos ambience. No stock watermarks, no other brands in shot.
